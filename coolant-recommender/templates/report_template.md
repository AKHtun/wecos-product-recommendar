## 5. Mandatory Warnings (every coolant recommendation)

> **Drain the previous coolant completely and flush with demineralized water before charging the new product. Do NOT mix coolant technologies (IAT / OAT / HOAT / NOAT / Si-OAT) — even similar colours can be chemically incompatible and form gel, sludge or corrosion.**

> **Topping-up restriction:** If the customer must top up between scheduled changes, top up only with the recommended new coolant or with demineralized water, never with a different brand/technology.
