## 5. Mandatory Warnings

> **Tank changeover:** Before switching diesel grades, run the existing tank as low as practical. Avoid mixing additized premium diesel with non-additized fuel mid-tank — the detergent package may dissolve existing tank-bottom deposits and overload the fuel filter on first fill. Replace the fuel filter after the first full tank of the new grade.
